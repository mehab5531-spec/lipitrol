What is Lipitrol?

Lipitrol is an oral suspension containing atorvastatin (a statin). It is used to help lower LDL ("bad") cholesterol and triglycerides in the blood, as part of a treatment plan set by your doctor.

How does Lipitrol work?

The liver makes most of the body's cholesterol, which makes it the main target of Lipitrol.

Reduces production

Lowers the amount of cholesterol made in the liver.

Increases clearance

Helps the liver remove more LDL cholesterol from the blood.

Benefits of Lipitrol

✔ Lowers LDL

Reduces "bad" cholesterol in the blood.

✔ Lowers triglycerides

Reduces triglyceride levels in the blood.

✔ Protects the heart

Helps reduce the risk of heart and blood vessel disease.

Obesity & Cholesterol

Excess weight and obesity can lead to:

  • Higher LDL ("bad") cholesterol
  • Higher triglycerides in the blood
  • Greater risk of heart and blood vessel disease

High cholesterol and fats can build up inside the arteries. Treatment together with a healthy lifestyle helps reduce this risk.

Thyroid Disorders & Their Effect

The thyroid gland affects cholesterol levels and body weight.

Healthy Tips

Exercise regularly
Drink plenty of water

Important Information

Note: This page is for awareness only and does not replace advice from your doctor or pharmacist. Do not start, stop, or change your Lipitrol dose unless your doctor tells you to, and read the package leaflet before use. Lipitrol is not a weight-loss medicine; it works best together with a healthy diet and regular physical activity.
